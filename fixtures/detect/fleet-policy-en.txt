6. GENERAL CONDITIONS (SUMMARY)

6.1 The insured must keep each vehicle in safe operating condition and comply with the Highway Traffic
Act and the Commercial Vehicle Operator's Registration (CVOR) requirements. Any change to the CVOR
safety rating must be reported to the broker within 10 days.

6.2 Vehicles may not be used to carry passengers for compensation, dangerous goods requiring a
placard, or loads outside the declared radius of operation without the insurer's written consent.

6.3 The insured must report every accident to the broker as soon as practicable and in any event within
seven days, and must cooperate with the insurer's investigation, including providing dashcam and
electronic logging device data where available.

6.4 The insurer may inspect any vehicle and the insured's maintenance records on reasonable notice.
Maintenance records must be kept for at least two years and include annual safety inspections.

6.5 This policy may be cancelled by the insured at any time on written notice, or by the insurer on 15
days' notice by registered mail or 5 days' notice if delivered personally. Return premium on
cancellation by the insured is calculated on the short rate table.

6.6 Statutory conditions under the Insurance Act (Ontario) form part of this policy. In case of
conflict between this summary and the policy wording, the policy wording prevails.

6.7 Territory: this policy covers the described automobiles while operated in Canada and the
continental United States. Trips into the United States require proof of the federal minimum
financial responsibility filing, which the insurer will issue on request for each trip or annually.

6.8 Rating information: premiums are based on the drivers, vehicles, radius and annual distance
declared in this schedule. The insured must report any change in operations, a new terminal, a new
regular route or an increase of more than 20% in annual distance within 30 days. The insurer may
adjust the premium from the date of the change.

6.9 Subrogation: after paying a claim the insurer takes over the insured's rights of recovery against
any person responsible for the loss. The insured must do nothing after a loss to prejudice these
rights, and must sign any document reasonably required to exercise them.

6.10 Other insurance: when other valid insurance covers the same loss, this policy pays its rateable
share, except for non-owned automobiles, where it is excess over any other insurance available to the
driver or owner.
